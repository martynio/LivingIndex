# Methodology and interpretation

**Status: principles established; detailed calculation contract pending research.**

Living Index will keep a verifiable distinction between:

1. **Observed source measures** — released statistical values with precise provenance.
2. **Derived measures** — reproducible calculations with named inputs and transformations.
3. **Reconstructions / estimates** — explicitly qualified methods with uncertainty and limitations.
4. **Unavailable / incomparable values** — missing results, never silently assigned zero.

## Financial comparisons

- A worker's average gross wage is not a household's disposable net income.
- Mean and median answer different questions and cannot be swapped silently.
- Local historical currencies require correct treatment of euro adoption and redenomination.
- Market exchange rates and purchasing-power parity answer different questions.
- A "money remaining" balance requires compatible income and expense definitions.

## Working-time equivalent

When consistent net earnings and paid working hours exist:

`net hourly earnings = monthly net employment income / monthly paid hours`

`working-hour equivalent of an expense = expense / net hourly earnings`

This is **not** equivalent to personal free time, commuting time, or unpaid work. An explicitly gross-based indicator must not be labeled as net time.

## Life satisfaction

A survey average on a 0–10 scale must not be reported as the percentage of respondents satisfied with life. Any future calculated Living Index score must remain distinct from observed survey responses.

## Presentation rules

Every published value needs source, dataset ID, population, period, measure, unit, processing method, version and quality status. Labels, written warnings and accessible details accompany visual markers.

Detailed data contracts, approved formulas, and quality tests are **future research/decision outputs** — not invented in this bootstrap.
